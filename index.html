Happy 14th monthsary! It feels surreal that we have already shared 14 beautiful months together. They say time moves fast when you are happy, but with you, every single day has been filled with comfort, genuine laughter, and memories that I treasure deeply in my heart.

Thank you for being my peace after long days, my favorite conversation at any hour, and the person who understands me effortlessly. Thank you for your warmth, your understanding, your sweet gestures, and the way your presence simply brightens up everything around you.

Fourteen months is a reminder of how much we have grown together—learning each other's quirks, cheering on each other's small victories, and holding hands through every quiet and noisy chapter. I promise to keep choosing you, loving you more each day, and standing by you through whatever comes our way.

"In all the world, there is no heart for me like yours. In all the world, there is no love for you like mine."

Forever and always yours,
